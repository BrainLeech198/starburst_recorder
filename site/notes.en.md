A local-first personal recorder with built-in AI: jot down today and AI turns it into a full diary entry. No sign-up, no cloud — everything stays on your device. AI runs on your own model and key.

## What's new

- **Three Finance views**: switch between day, month and year; paging moves one day, one month or one year at a time, and the view keeps the date you were on.
- **A whole year at a glance**: the yearly view lays out income, spending and balance for all twelve months — click a month to return to its entries.
- **The weight trend is now clickable and readable**: tap a dot to see its date and weight, and the day panel below follows; on the home card each dot opens that exact day.
- **Charts carry their numbers**: high / middle / low gridlines, a value on every point when the range is short, high and low labels when it is dense, and first / middle / last dates along the axis.
- **No more shrinking text on small phones**: chart numbers use a fixed-size HTML layer, so they stay legible on narrow screens.

## Download

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10/11 | `starburst-recorder-0.11.1-setup.exe` (~96 MB) |
| Android 10+ | `starburst-recorder-0.11.1.apk` (~62 MB, release-signed) |

Android is sideloaded: allow "install unknown apps". If you previously installed a debug build, uninstall it first (different signature — the upgrade install will fail).
Storage follows the platform: SQLite on desktop, IndexedDB in browsers and WebViews.

## Known limitations

0.11.1 is still an early build, and the data format may change between versions — export a backup of anything you care about before upgrading. Assistant conversations and memories stay on this device and are not part of backups. Health Connect import on Android is experimental and not yet verified on a real device; tell us if it cannot read anything.
