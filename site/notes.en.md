A local-first personal recorder with built-in AI: jot down today and AI turns it into a full diary entry. No sign-up, no cloud — everything stays on your device. AI runs on your own model and key.

## What's new

- **Food photos while logging intake**: photos from the food library now appear in the picker of "Log intake"; tap a thumbnail to open it full screen and swipe through the photos; portion and meal-component lists carry thumbnails too
- **A clearer word on health numbers**: TDEE, estimated TDEE and every food's calories are estimates. The health page explains this once on first visit, and a small note stays beside the intake card, weigh-in card, body profile and AI answers — tap it for the full explanation. Your real metabolism is a matter for a check-up and your doctor
- **The three health sub-pages now match the diary / notes toolbar**: intake, sleep and weight actions are equal-width filled buttons in two columns; when the count is odd the trash link spans the full row, so narrow phones no longer show ragged rows with an empty slot

## Download

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10/11 | `starburst-recorder-0.5.0-setup.exe` (~95 MB) |
| Android 10+ | `starburst-recorder-0.5.0.apk` (~16 MB, release-signed) |

Android is sideloaded: allow "install unknown apps". If you previously installed a debug build, uninstall it first (different signature — the upgrade install will fail).
Storage follows the platform: SQLite on desktop, IndexedDB in browsers and WebViews.

## Known limitations

0.5.0 is an early build. Finance and calendar are still placeholder pages, and the data format may change between versions — export a backup of anything you care about before upgrading.
