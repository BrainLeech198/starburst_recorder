A local-first personal recorder with built-in AI: jot down today and AI turns it into a full diary entry. No sign-up, no cloud — everything stays on your device. AI runs on your own model and key.

## What's new

- **Weight trend gets its own page**: open it from the chart on the health home; switch between 7 / 30 / 90 days, tap a point or a record to inspect a day, and see range change, average weight and a regression-based weekly rate
- **More health metrics on the trend page**: average intake, average sleep, protein-goal days and estimated TDEE for the range; the selected day also shows energy gap, protein progress and the difference from ideal sleep
- **Two ways to edit an intake**: the edit entry asks whether to change only this entry or also update the food library, with the edited values converted back to per 100 g
- **Meals have thumbnails**: the meal form shows a cover and component thumbnails, and the selected list in "Log an intake" now sits on its own layer
- **Both add-food entries match Confirm**: "Add a food" and "From one sentence" now use the same solid primary button

## Download

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10/11 | `starburst-recorder-0.7.0-setup.exe` (~95 MB) |
| Android 10+ | `starburst-recorder-0.7.0.apk` (~16 MB, release-signed) |

Android is sideloaded: allow "install unknown apps". If you previously installed a debug build, uninstall it first (different signature — the upgrade install will fail).
Storage follows the platform: SQLite on desktop, IndexedDB in browsers and WebViews.

## Known limitations

0.7.0 is an early build. Finance and calendar are still placeholder pages, and the data format may change between versions — export a backup of anything you care about before upgrading.
