A local-first personal recorder with built-in AI: jot down today and AI turns it into a full diary entry. No sign-up, no cloud — everything stays on your device. AI runs on your own model and key.

## What's new

- **Intake entries can be edited again**: tap ✎ in the list or edit from the detail view; regular foods let you change name, time, portion and nutrition, while meals recalculate proportionally from component grams
- **Day switching now has motion**: diary, health home, intake, weight and sleep pages slide in by direction; elder-care reduced motion or the system setting turns it off
- **Easier back navigation**: the back buttons on the intake, sleep and weight pages moved to the left; the finance data foundation is in place, with the monthly ledger UI coming next

## Download

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10/11 | `starburst-recorder-0.6.2-setup.exe` (~95 MB) |
| Android 10+ | `starburst-recorder-0.6.2.apk` (~16 MB, release-signed) |

Android is sideloaded: allow "install unknown apps". If you previously installed a debug build, uninstall it first (different signature — the upgrade install will fail).
Storage follows the platform: SQLite on desktop, IndexedDB in browsers and WebViews.

## Known limitations

0.6.2 is an early build. Finance and calendar are still placeholder pages, and the data format may change between versions — export a backup of anything you care about before upgrading.
