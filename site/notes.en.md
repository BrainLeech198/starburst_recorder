A local-first personal recorder with built-in AI: jot down today and AI turns it into a full diary entry. No sign-up, no cloud — everything stays on your device. AI runs on your own model and key.

## What's new

- **The calendar finally works**: the month grid blends diary, health and finance counts into a single shade, so you can see at a glance which days you filled in. Click any cell to open the day page, where diary, notes, food, sleep, training, finance and schedules line up by time; days with schedules carry a small dot.
- **Schedules and recurring tasks**: one-off, daily or weekly repeats, with a time and an optional reminder. Desktop shows a system notification while the app is open, Android pre-schedules local notifications, and deleted items go to a trash you can restore from or empty.
- **Week view**: all seven days in one column, showing what is planned, what is still unchecked, how many records each day holds and what it cost — and you can add a schedule to any day right there.
- **Monthly review**: days with records, entries written, income and spending, average sleep, training and food, plus a small per-day grid. Recurring-task completion rate and streaks live here too.
- **AI weekly and monthly reports**: hand a week or a month of records to your own model and get an editable review you can copy or save straight into today's diary.

## Download

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10/11 | `starburst-recorder-0.11.0-setup.exe` (~96 MB) |
| Android 10+ | `starburst-recorder-0.11.0.apk` (~62 MB, release-signed) |

Android is sideloaded: allow "install unknown apps". If you previously installed a debug build, uninstall it first (different signature — the upgrade install will fail).
Storage follows the platform: SQLite on desktop, IndexedDB in browsers and WebViews.

## Known limitations

0.11.0 is still an early build, and the data format may change between versions — export a backup of anything you care about before upgrading. Assistant conversations and memories stay on this device and are not part of backups. Health Connect import on Android is experimental and not yet verified on a real device; tell us if it cannot read anything.
